Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The Infinite Attraction 888 Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the Infinite Attraction 888 YouTube channel. It is used only to upload videos to that channel and to manage that channel's video titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, and branding.

Data accessed

With the channel owner's authorization, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to access and manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not collect data about any other person.

Data storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored on the operator's computer and are used only to run this tool. No data is sold, shared, rented, or transferred to any third party. No advertising or analytics services are used.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke this tool's access at any time from the Google account permissions page (myaccount.google.com, Third-party access).

Contact

Questions can be sent to the channel owner through the Infinite Attraction 888 YouTube channel page.
